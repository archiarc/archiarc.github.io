What It TakesA guide for asset holders

A data center is not a building. It is an agreement between power, land, capital and demand.

Each element must be secured, and in the right order. These are the questions every project has to answer before it can stand.

  1. I

    AssessmentIs the asset real?

    Power that exists on paper is not always power that can be delivered. The first task is to establish what the land, the power source and the surrounding conditions can truly support, and when.

  2. II

    DirectionWhat should it become?

    Not every site suits every use. Scale, power profile, climate and connectivity point toward a particular kind of facility and a particular kind of customer.

  3. III

    DemandWho will use it?

    Investors finance contracts, not buildings. A credible customer commitment is often the moment a project becomes real.

  4. IV

    Design & costWhat will it truly cost?

    Generation, cooling and equipment lead times shape the budget long before ground is broken. Estimates must be tested against real offers.

  5. V

    ApprovalsIs it permitted?

    Land use, power generation, environmental and construction approvals each follow their own sequence. Starting late is one of the most common causes of delay.

  6. VI

    CapitalHow is it financed?

    The structure of ownership, equity and debt decides who controls the project and who shares in its value.

  7. VII

    DeliveryCan it be built and run?

    Construction, commissioning and operation need people on site who answer to the owner rather than to the contractor.
